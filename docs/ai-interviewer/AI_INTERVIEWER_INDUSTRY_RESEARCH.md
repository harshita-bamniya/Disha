# AI First-Round Interviewer: Industry Research for BeginablAI

Access date for all sources: **2026-10-10**. Research only; no application code touched.

**Claim labels:** [Vendor-claim] = vendor's own page/PR; [Independent] = academic, journalist, analyst, or third-party review (third-party price aggregators are labelled [Independent] but are *estimates*); [Regulatory/legal] = statute, regulator, or law-firm summary of law; [Unknown/unverified] = could not confirm from a primary source.

**Method caveat:** Research was done through web search summaries plus a handful of direct page fetches (Gemini pricing, Groq STT docs, Deepgram pricing, OpenAI data-controls, Anthropic retention, Gemini API terms). Many vendor and law-firm pages were seen only via search snippets, not read in full. Anything price- or date-sensitive should be re-checked on the live page before use. Nothing here is legal advice.

## 1. Executive summary

1. **The market has converged on three modes**: (a) asynchronous recorded video/audio answers (HireVue, Spark Hire, myInterview, Interviewer.AI); (b) conversational AI voice/chat agents that ask adaptive follow-ups (Alex/Apriora, micro1 Zara, Mercor, Ribbon, Sapia chat, Paradox for scheduling/screening); (c) coding/skills-simulation with proctoring (HackerRank, CodeSignal, Vervoe). Greenhouse itself now sells a voice-AI screener. The ATS layer (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) is where results land as scorecards/stage activity.
2. **The evidence base favours structure, not "AI magic".** Structured interviews have the best mean validity among common selection methods (~.42 corrected, wide credibility interval .18-.66; Sackett et al. 2022). Evidence that *AI scoring* adds validity beyond a well-built rubric is mostly vendor-produced; independent work (Hickman et al.) shows ML scoring can work for some constructs but ASR transcription errors differ by demographic group.
3. **The defensible design is: standard questions per role -> LLM-scored against a written rubric with evidence quotes -> human decides.** Every serious vendor (micro1, Greenhouse, Sapia, HireVue) markets "human in the loop" or "your team decides".
4. **Avoid face/voice-emotion/personality inference.** HireVue dropped visual analysis (2020 internal, announced Jan 2021) after an EPIC FTC complaint; EU AI Act bans emotion inference at work/education (applied 2 Feb 2025); Illinois, Maryland and California have face-/video-specific rules; an ACLU complaint (Mar 2025) targets ASR bias against deaf and Indigenous speakers. Score **content of answers against a rubric**, never face, tone, or "personality".
5. **Candidate drop-off is a real product risk.** A Greenhouse-sourced 2026 report says ~38% of candidates withdrew from a process because of an AI interview [Independent/anecdotal-survey, vendor-adjacent]; other (vendor) studies say candidates like chat/AI interviews. Evidence is conflicting and mostly vendor-funded. Design for transparency, retake/practice, accommodation, and fast feedback.
6. **India-specific:** DPDP Rules were notified Nov 2025; the substantive consent/notice/children/retention duties start ~13-14 May 2027 (sources differ by a day). Build consent, purpose-limited retention, grievance contact, and verifiable-parental-consent (age gate 18+) in from day one. UPSC aspirants include many under-25s but the product should be 18+ only.
7. **LLM provider choice matters for candidate PII:** use paid-tier Gemini (free tier trains on and may human-review your data), enable Groq ZDR, and get DPAs. See section 9.
8. **Unit economics are small:** per Section 9, a 20-minute interview costs on the order of a few US cents to ~$0.50 in STT + LLM, depending on stack (our estimate from listed prices; assumptions stated). The expensive parts are engineering, integrity tooling and support, not tokens. Competitor public per-interview prices range from ~$0.50/min (myInterview) to ~$2-4/interview (Ribbon overage) to $5-30+ (HireVue estimates, unverified).

## 3. Per-platform notes (condensed; dimensions not found are "not publicly documented")

**HireVue / Modern Hire.** HireVue acquired Modern Hire (closed 3 May 2023; ~1,150 combined customers; terms undisclosed) [Independent: [William Blair](https://www.williamblair.com/News/Modern-and-HireVue-Transaction), [Hunt Scanlon](https://huntscanlon.com/hirevue-acquires-modern-hire/)]. Facial analysis: SHRM/EPIC report it was dropped (stopped early 2020; announced Jan 2021) after internal research found language analysis made visual redundant, amid an EPIC FTC complaint; legacy models may have kept facial features for a time [Independent: [SHRM](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition/hirevue-discontinues-facial-analysis-screening), [EPIC](https://epic.org/hirevue-facing-ftc-complaint-from-epic-halts-use-of-facial-recognition/), [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90597594/ai-algorithm-auditing-hirevue)] - verified. ## 4. Common industry patterns

- **Role-config = template + competencies**: job -> competency model/skill list -> question set -> rubric (HireVue, micro1 "skills", Greenhouse interview kits).
- **Output = structured scorecard** with ratings, evidence/transcript, recruiter summary, and a ranked/tiered shortlist; ATS write-back as stage result or scorecard.
- **Human-in-the-loop framing** is universal; auto-reject is avoided in marketing (also reduces GDPR Art.22/AI Act exposure).
- **Integrity stack**: ID/selfie checks, browser focus/tab flags, similarity checks across candidates, replay review, behavioural signals. Vendor-reported cheating rates vary wildly (HireVue <1% similarity-flagged vs 38.5% "AI-cheating behaviour" from an integrity vendor's live-interview data [Vendor-claim: [Fabric](https://fabrichq.ai/blogs/state-of-cheating-in-interviews-in-2026-tools-trends-and-prevention)]) - treat all as unverified.
- **Pricing models**: per-seat/subscription (Spark Hire, CodeSignal, HackerRank), credits/attempts with $20 overage (CodeSignal, HackerRank), per-interview bundles (Ribbon, HireQuotient, myInterview per-minute), enterprise quote (HireVue, Paradox, Alex).
- **ATS integration = webhook/stage trigger + API write-back of result link/score** (Greenhouse partner API, Lever webhooks, Ashby activity).

## 5. Essential for initial release vs later

**Initial release (MVP):**
1. Employer creates interview template per job: title/JD -> 4-8 competencies -> LLM-drafted questions that employer edits and approves; same question set for all candidates (structured).
2. Candidate flow: consent + notice screen (AI use, what is scored, retention, human review, grievance contact), device check, text or audio answers (audio -> STT), time limits, one practice question, retry on technical failure, accommodation/alternative-format request link, English plus Hindi if feasible.
3. Scoring: LLM scores each answer against a written 1-5 behaviourally anchored rubric, returns evidence quotes and confidence; **content only** (no face/voice emotion/personality).
4. Recruiter report: per-competency scores, quotes, red flags, summary, transcript, human override/notes, shortlist status. Never auto-reject.
5. Audit trail: model/prompt/rubric version per score, who changed what, data export + deletion.
6. Integrity v1: unique link, session logging, copy-paste/tab-focus flags, answer-similarity across candidates, follow-up probes on specifics; flags are advisory.
7. Compliance basics: DPDP notice/consent, retention timer, paid-tier LLM with DPA, adverse-impact monitoring dashboard (selection rates by available group data).

**Later:** live conversational voice with adaptive follow-ups; ATS connectors (start with webhooks + CSV/API; then Greenhouse partner API, Lever, Ashby); ID verification and liveness; code/case simulations; candidate feedback reports; calibration against hired-performance data; multilingual STT with per-language WER monitoring; bias audits by third party; employer-side rubric libraries.

## 6. Differentiation opportunities for an India/UPSC-focused product

(Our inferences, not sourced facts.)
- **Civil-services competency frameworks**: ethics/integrity case studies (GS-4 style), administrative judgement, writing/essay structure, current-affairs reasoning, DAF/background-based questioning, panel-interview style practice - none of the surveyed vendors documents these.
- **Dual-sided value**: same engine as candidate practice (BeginablAI already has aspirants) plus employer screening; candidate-facing feedback is a known differentiator (Sapia) and lowers drop-off.
- **Indian languages and accents**: Hindi/Hinglish STT with per-language accuracy reporting; accommodations for speech/hearing differences (the ACLU complaint shows the ASR risk).
- **Roles outside tech**: policy research, think-tank/NGO, CSR, public-affairs, ed-tech; competitors skew to coding or hourly hiring.
- **DPDP-native**: consent manager readiness, India data residency options, grievance workflow, plain-language notice in regional languages.
- **Price**: transparent per-interview or per-job pricing in INR (most vendors hide pricing).

## 7. Risks and lessons

- **Mobley v. Workday (N.D. Cal. 3:23-cv-00770):** ADEA collective conditionally certified 16 May 2025 on the theory that Workday's AI screening acts as a unified policy; court rejected that variation among customer employers defeats certification; notice/opt-in activity reported in early 2026; later rulings and discovery orders reported but I could not verify against the docket [Independent/law-firm: [Fisher Phillips](https://www.fisherphillips.com/en/insights/insights/discrimination-lawsuit-over-workdays-ai-hiring-tools-can-proceed-as-class-action-6-things), [Fennemore](https://www.fennemorelaw.com/a-new-storm-for-employers-may-be-forming-in-the-california-sunshine-preliminary-certification-of-collective-action-against-human-resource-ai-software-provider/)]. Lesson: the *vendor* can be treated as an agent of the employer; keep adverse-impact logs and documentation. California regs (effective 1 Oct 2025) also treat agents as employers and require 4-year ADS data retention [Regulatory: [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/ja/insights/publications/2025/08/california-adopts-new-employment-ai-regulations-effective-october-1-2025)].
- **ASR bias:** Hickman, Langer et al. (J. Applied Psychology 2025, doi 10.1037/apl0001247) study transcription accuracy and demographics on automated interview scoring [Independent: [summary](https://academicnewsletter.sufe.edu.cn/info/368799)]; ACLU complaint alleges the same failure mode. Mitigate: report WER by accent/language, allow typed answers, human review.
- **Facial/voice analysis**: HireVue's retreat; EU ban on workplace emotion inference; Illinois/Maryland face-specific consent. Do not build it.
- **Candidate drop-off**: ~38% withdrawal claim (Greenhouse-sourced via [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/04/4-in-10-job-candidates-bailed-hiring-rounds-required-ai-interview/?rand=8593)) vs Chicago Booth/PSG finding that 78% chose the AI interviewer when given the choice [Independent per press; PSG is a vendor partner] vs vendor satisfaction scores. All of this is survey/anecdotal; no Reddit/HN/Glassdoor threads were retrieved in my searches, so *sentiment from those forums is not documented here*. Offer a human alternative, short interviews, clear timelines.
- **LLM cheating and fake candidates**: Gartner's "1 in 4 candidate profiles fake by 2028" is widely repeated secondhand [Unverified original]; KnowBe4 North Korean operative case widely reported; vendor stats are self-interested. Mitigate with unique/variant questions, specificity probes, ID checks at later stages; do not auto-reject on integrity flags.
- **Explainability**: HireVue critique shows opaque statements invite criticism; evidence quotes per score are the practical answer.
- **Validity overclaim:** Structured interview mean validity ~.42 (80% CI .18-.66) [Independent: [Sackett et al. 2022 via summaries](https://www.siop.org/tip-article/is-cognitive-ability-the-best-predictor-of-job-performance); [Huffcutt & Murphy commentary](https://resolve.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/7CB1F7C86CB0D15328B3F07AD5F964E2/S1754942623000421a.pdf/structured-interviews-moving-beyond-mean-validity.pdf)]; unstructured ~.19. These are for human-rated structured interviews; **I found no independent peer-reviewed evidence that LLM-scored interviews match that**. Hickman et al. 2022 (N=1,073 mock interviews) found ML could score Big Five traits in mock settings; not the same as job-performance validity [Independent: [Virginia Tech](https://psyc.vt.edu/latest-news/louis-hickman-ai-algorithms.html)]. Plan a local validation study.
